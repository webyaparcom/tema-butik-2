Gaziantep, September 2026

Dear reader,

This issue again has only a few pieces, because we want to fit each one with you. The boutique’s four promises are below.

  1. 01

    Natural fabric.

    Linen, wool and silk; no piece reaches the rail without its fabric content on the label.

  2. 02

    Every piece with a fitting.

    Try it on at the boutique before you buy; your measurements go onto a written card.

  3. 03

    The first alteration is on us.

    Hem, sleeve and waist alterations are done in the workroom the same week.

  4. 04

    Few pieces, long life.

    At most twelve designs per issue; a sold-out design is not made again.

Nesrin Aral

Founder and head tailor (fictional)
